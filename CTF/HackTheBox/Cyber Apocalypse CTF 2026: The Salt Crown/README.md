## 📖 About

This repository documents my participation in **Hack The Box's Cyber Apocalypse CTF 2026: The Salt Crown**, one of the largest annual cybersecurity Capture The Flag competitions.

The event focuses on real-world offensive security concepts through challenges covering reverse engineering, binary exploitation, web security, cryptography, digital forensics, hardware, AI, cloud, and many other domains.

Rather than simply collecting flags, this repository serves as a personal knowledge base where I document my methodology, tools, mistakes, and lessons learned throughout the competition.

## ⚔️ Story

When High King Maelor attempted to steal the realm's oldest vow, the Brine Signet shattered into fragments, plunging Valyssar into chaos.

Trust has collapsed. Forged decrees spread across kingdoms while the mysterious **Quiet Marches** advance relentlessly under the command of Alyss.

The ultimate objective is the creation of **The Salt Crown**, a fault-tolerant system that permanently limits unchecked authority.

In this competition, the battlefield isn't swords or magic.

It's reverse engineering, exploitation, cryptography, hardware, infrastructure, and security research.
